# Better Items changelog

## 1.0 — First release (PC)

### What it is
- A standalone item icon mod for PokeMMO. It refines the game's own item art instead of replacing it: same designs, same colours, just cleaner, bigger and easier to read.
- Works with any theme (including Better Default) and with no theme mod at all.
- **Two versions — install only one:**
  - **Better Items:** a clean dark outline.
  - **Better Items White Outline:** the same icons with a 1 px white line around them, including inside larger holes like rings, loops and wheels, for extra readability on dark windows.

### Visual improvements
- **Bigger icons:** the original art is enlarged to fill the icon box. Poké Balls go from about 16 px to 23 px.
- **Cleaner edges:** curves and diagonals are smoother instead of blocky.
- **Smooth shading** inside each colour area, while small details stay crisp: letters, highlights, buttons and dots.
- **Solid outline** and slightly higher contrast, so every icon reads well on dark windows.
- **Line art** (bikes, fishing rods, strings and frames) gets a crisper treatment.
- **Light and gold outlines are kept:** icons that have their own gold ring, white edge or rainbow edge retain them.

### Coverage
- **All 2,366 item icon files** (1,140 different images), with the game's own file names and item IDs.
- The game's own ocarinas are included. The -1 debug marker is left unchanged.
- Every icon stays 24 × 24 px (the few odd-sized icons keep their own size).

### UI scale
- Made for **1.0× UI**, and tested at **1.25×, 1.5×, 1.75× and 2.0×**. The game scales the icons itself, keeping the bag, bag tabs and hotbar at the right size.

### Tested in game
- All 8 Bag pockets (Items, Medicine, Battle Items, Key Items, Poké Balls, TMs & HMs, Berries, Cosmetics), the bag tab icons and the hotbar.
- UI scale 1.0×, 1.25×, 1.5×, 1.75× and 2.0×.
- With and without Dynamic Ocarinas.

### Known issues
- **White Outline:** fishing rods and string-like items (necklaces, ropes) can look a bit loopy, because the white line follows both sides of the thin fishing line or string.
- **White Outline:** the 22 icons that already filled the whole box (zodiac cards, big chests, etc.) are drawn slightly smaller so the white line fits.
- Android: not tested yet.
- Not checked yet: shops, GTL, Battle Bag, PC and trade windows.
